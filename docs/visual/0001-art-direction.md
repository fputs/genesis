## Decision
Hybrid style. Dark tactical structure for readability, manuscript ornament for identity.

## Style pillars
- **Chrome:** dark charcoal/slate surfaces, flat, restrained.
- **Ornament:** thin gold-leaf hairline borders; small corner knots. Ornament frames, never clutters.
- **Headers:** illuminated drop-capital (vermilion + gold) on major titles only, then serif small caps.
- **Body text:** clean, highly readable; parchment off-white on slate.
- **Map:** hand-inked line art on a parchment wash; crisp grid.
- **Tokens:** round portraits in wax-seal rims (party vs. enemy distinguished by rim color).
- **Accent:** one cool modern accent (cyan) reserved for interactive highlights.
- **Palette direction:** slate, parchment, gold, vermilion, cyan. Exact tokens TBD.

## Options considered
Illuminated manuscript, dark tactical, painterly fantasy, pixel CRPG. Rejected as pure styles:
manuscript fatigues in long editor sessions; tactical alone is generic; painterly is art-expensive; pixel cramps at high res.
