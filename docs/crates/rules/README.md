# rules

Pathfinder Remaster procedures for one encounter. No Bevy types, no rendering, no file I/O.

Callers inject dice through `Dice`. `SeededDice` is the gameplay stream. `ScriptedDice` is the test stream.

## Surface

- `prepare_combatant` copies a `CreatureTemplate` through `apply_effects` into derived stats (HP, Armor Class, Perception, Speed, Strike) and the class feature list. The only feature is `ClassFeature::ReactiveStrike`. Shield Block is a separate bool, not a class feature. A shield, when the creature has one, copies hardness, hit points, maximum hit points, and broken threshold, with `raised` clear. `stealth_dc` copies through. Absent means that creature cannot be Hidden or Undetected. The Hall Guard and the fighters leave it absent.
- `start_encounter` requires four party templates, places everyone on a `Grid` of 5-foot squares, and rolls Perception initiative.
- `legal_actions` lists `Action::Stride { destination }`, `Action::Step { destination }`, `Action::Strike { target }`, `Action::RaiseShield`, `Action::DropProne`, `Action::Stand`, `Action::Crawl { destination }`, `Action::Leap { destination }`, `Action::Seek`, `Action::PointOut { target }`, and `Action::TakeCover` for the active conscious combatant. Raise a Shield is legal only when that creature is wielding a shield that is not broken and not destroyed. Drop Prone is legal only when the creature is not already prone. Stand is legal only when the creature is prone. While prone, Stride, Step, and Leap are absent, and Crawl and Stand are the movement options. Crawl is one square whose enter cost is at most 5 feet. Leap is absent when Speed is below 15. Seek is legal whenever the creature can act. Point Out is legal for each creature the actor Observes when at least one ally has that creature Undetected. Take Cover is legal when the creature is prone, adjacent to an unwalkable square, or would already have cover against at least one enemy. Unconscious and dead creatures have no actions. A Strike may target an unconscious opponent, including one who is Hidden or Concealed. A Strike may not target a dead one, or one who is Undetected by the attacker. Nothing is legal while a Shield Block offer is open.
- `declare_action` spends one action. A Stride moves up to Speed. Each square it leaves is checked, in order, for opponents who have Reactive Strike, a ready reaction, and that square in reach. Those reactors go in `CombatantId` order. Each rolls at multiple attack penalty 0, spends that reaction, and does not change the strike count. The event is emitted before the mover enters the next square. At 0 HP the mover stays on the square they were leaving and later squares are skipped. Crawl and Leap use that same leave-square path. A Step moves 5 feet when Speed is at least 10 and the destination is normal terrain. It does not change the strike count and does not provoke. A Strike rolls against Armor Class. The multiple attack penalty is −5 / −10 on later Strikes that turn. A prone attacker also takes a −2 circumstance penalty. Those circumstance penalties do not stack; the more severe one applies. Off-guard remains a −2 circumstance penalty to Armor Class and applies together with the attacker's penalty. A Strike does not provoke. Step and Strike do not spend a reaction. Raise a Shield sets `raised` until the start of that creature's next turn. Drop Prone sets prone and does not move or provoke. Stand clears prone and does not move or provoke.
- A square is normal, difficult, or greater. The default is normal. `Grid::set_terrain` marks a square; the room-one hall never does, so every hall square stays normal. Entering a difficult square costs 5 extra feet on top of that square's foot cost. Entering a greater difficult square costs 10 extra feet. Step is illegal on difficult or greater terrain. Crawl cannot enter a square whose total cost is above 5. Leap uses its own cap, 10 feet at Speed 15 and 15 feet at Speed 30, measured with the same foot-cost rule. It does not enter another conscious creature's square. It can land on difficult terrain.
- A raised shield that is neither broken nor destroyed gives a +2 circumstance bonus to Armor Class. Broken and destroyed shields do not. Cover is the other circumstance bonus. The strike uses the larger of the shield bonus and the cover bonus and does not add them. The start of the creature's turn clears `raised` and `taking_cover` before any action.
- Detection is per observer and subject. Observed is not stored. A missing pair means Observed. `set_detection` stores Concealed, Hidden, or Undetected, and refuses Hidden or Undetected when the subject has no Stealth DC. Seek rolls d20 plus Perception against each Hidden or Undetected enemy who has a Stealth DC, in `CombatantId` order. Critical success makes them Observed. Success turns Undetected into Hidden and Hidden into Observed, and leaves Concealed unchanged. Failure and critical failure change nothing. The degree helper applies, including a natural 20 and a natural 1. Point Out spends one action and changes Undetected to Hidden for allies only.
- A Strike against a Concealed creature rolls a DC 5 flat check through `Dice` before the attack roll. Against a Hidden creature the flat check is DC 11. The flat check is a separate roll and comes first. A failed flat check misses, spends the action, and does not increase the strike count. A passed flat check continues into the normal attack roll.
- Cover is derived per attack along the segment from the attacker's square to the target's square, using the same center-line test as flanking. Another creature's square on that segment is lesser cover, +1. An unwalkable square on that segment is standard cover, +2. If both apply, the bonus is +2. The attacker and the target do not grant cover to themselves. Take Cover sets `taking_cover`. While that flag is set, derived cover improves one step: no cover becomes +1 only when the creature is prone or adjacent to an unwalkable square, lesser becomes +2, standard becomes +4, and +4 stays +4. The flag clears when that creature Strides, Steps, Crawls, Leaps, Drops Prone, Stands, or Strikes.
- Shield Block is a reaction. It is legal when the creature has `shield_block`, the shield is raised, the shield is not broken and not destroyed, and the reaction is ready. The trigger is they would take damage from a Strike, including a Reactive Strike. All strike damage is physical. The shield prevents damage up to its hardness. The creature and the shield each take the rest, `max(0, damage - hardness)`. Hit points at or below the broken threshold break the shield. 0 Hit Points destroys it. A broken shield loses the AC bonus and cannot be raised or used to Shield Block. The offer is `pending_shield_block`; `resolve_pending_shield_block` accepts or declines it. Accepting spends the same reaction Reactive Strike spends, so the other is unavailable until that creature's next turn.
- A Strike's Armor Class is 2 lower when the target is off-guard to that attacker. Off-guard means prone, unconscious, or flanked by that attacker. Those circumstance penalties do not stack with each other. They are on Armor Class, so they still apply when the attacker is prone. Two conscious enemies of the target flank when the target is inside both melee reaches and a line between their square centers crosses opposite sides or opposite corners of the target.
- A successful Strike against an unconscious creature becomes a critical success before damage. A hit that reduces positive HP to 0 drops the creature prone and sets dying to 1 plus wounded, or 2 plus wounded on a critical success, then increases wounded by 1. Damage against a creature that already has dying increases dying by 1, or by 2 on a critical success, and does not increase wounded. Damage against an unconscious creature who is not dying gives them dying again by that same 1 or 2 plus wounded. Dying of 4 or more kills. One hit that deals at least twice maximum HP, dealt while the creature had more than 0 HP, kills and does not also apply dying.
- `reactors_for_left_square` lists the reactors for one square a creature is leaving.
- `reaction_ready` reports the flag on a combatant. `start_encounter` sets it for every conscious creature, and the start of that creature's turn sets it again. `spend_reaction` clears it and returns `Illegal::NoReaction` when it is already clear.
- `advance_if_turn_done` / `end_turn` take the dice and walk the initiative order. A dying creature rolls a d20 flat check at DC 10 plus dying, then the turn passes if they still cannot act. Critical success lowers dying by 2, success by 1, failure raises it by 1, and critical failure raises it by 2. A natural 20 or 1 adjusts that degree. Dying 0 removes dying and leaves the creature unconscious, prone, and wounded. Dead creatures do not take turns. A party member stays in one of the four slots.
- The encounter ends in `Victory` when every enemy is dead, or `Defeat` when every party member is dead. A creature at 0 HP who is not dead does not end it.

`Encounter` owns the initiative order, the current index, combatants keyed by `CombatantId`, the grid, and the detection pairs.

## Fidelity notes

Knocked out, dying, Raise a Shield, Shield Block, Drop Prone, Stand, Crawl, Leap, difficult terrain, Seek, Point Out, Take Cover, and cover run as written in Player Core. The hall has no difficult or greater squares. The Hall Guard and the fighters have no Stealth DC. Initiative ties that need a GM, the basic actions this fight does not run, reaction rules this iteration does not run, and Reactive Strike disruption are recorded in `docs/research/fidelity-ledger.md`.
