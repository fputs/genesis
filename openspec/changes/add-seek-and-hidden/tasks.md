# Tasks

Each scenario in `specs/seek-and-hidden/spec.md` has exactly one test. The test name is the scenario title in snake_case. Spaces, hyphens, and apostrophes become `_`. Punctuation, including commas, is dropped. `Off-guard` in a title contributes `off_guard`. A test comment cites the Player Core section on that requirement. A test that does not roll builds a `ScriptedDice` and asserts the held face is unused. Do not edit `check.rs`, `modifiers.rs`, `combatant.rs`, `positioning.rs`, `encounter.rs`, or anything under `docs/designer/`. Do not declare `OffGuard`, `Cover`, `Vital`, `MoveAction`, `Side`, `ActionCost`, or `ActionTrait`. Import `OffGuard` and `MoveAction` from positioning, `Cover` from modifiers, and `Vital` from the dying types. The cost type in this module is `DetectionCost`. Do not start these tasks until `OffGuard`, `MoveAction`, and `Vital::Unconscious` / `Vital::Dying` are public in `crates/rules`. Add no dependency. Do not add a field to `Creature` or `CombatantState`. This change names no `v1-reference` file, so none is ported.

## 6. Hide

- [ ] 6.1 An observer needs standard cover, greater cover, or `Concealment::Concealed`. Lesser cover does not qualify. One concealed observer is enough: natural 8, modifier +7, DC 15, total 15, hidden from that observer, and the other observer stays observed. The error is not `RequirementNotMet`. An observer without cover stays observed when another has standard cover. Cite Player Core "Hide". Verify `cargo test -p rules -- one_observer_without_cover_is_left_observed concealment_from_one_observer_hides_from_that_observer` passes
- [ ] 6.2 Reject, in order after `NotConscious`, `DuplicateId`, `SameCreature`, `NoStealthDc`, `RequirementNotMet`, then `Overflow`, and draw no die. `Vital::Unconscious` with no Stealth DC is `NotConscious`, not `NoStealthDc`. No Stealth DC with lesser cover is `NoStealthDc`, not `RequirementNotMet`. Hiding from yourself twice is `DuplicateId`, not `SameCreature`. Perception `2147483638` is `Overflow` from `DcModifier`, not `PerceptionOutOfRange`. Stealth DC `i32::MIN` with standard cover, and Stealth DC `i32::MAX` with greater cover, are `Overflow`. Cite Player Core "Hide". ## 7. Cover loss and Strike

- [ ] 7.1 `refresh` is never implicit. It ends hidden when the subject has neither standard nor greater cover and is `NotConcealed`. Lesser cover does not keep hidden. Undetected stays. `note_action(Stride)` does not call `refresh`; hidden stays until the caller calls `refresh`. `note_strike(detections, striker, target)` reads `OffGuard::Yes` before it changes relations, then sets every observer who had the striker hidden or undetected to observed and keeps concealment. A pair whose subject is not the striker stays, including the other direction. An undetected Strike is off-guard, then the striker is observed (proposed DEV-43). Neither function rolls. Cite Player Core "Hide", "Hidden", and "Sneak". Verify `cargo test -p rules -- losing_cover_and_concealment_ends_hidden lesser_cover_does_not_keep_a_creature_hidden concealment_keeps_a_hidden_creature_hidden losing_cover_does_not_end_undetected a_hidden_strike_is_off_guard_then_every_observer_observes a_strike_does_not_flip_the_other_direction a_stride_does_not_refresh_hidden refresh_after_a_stride_ends_hidden an_undetected_strike_is_off_guard_then_the_striker_is_observed` passes
