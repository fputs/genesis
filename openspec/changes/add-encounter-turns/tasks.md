Each scenario has one test. The test name is the scenario title in snake_case: lowercase, spaces become underscores, and a minus sign before a number becomes the word `minus`. Other punctuation is dropped. Tests use `ScriptedDice` and cite the Player Core section on that scenario's requirement. Assert through the public functions. Do not call `attack_check_parts` from a scenario test.
